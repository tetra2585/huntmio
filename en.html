HUNTMIO Support

HUNTMIO lets you hide AR treasure chests in your room or a park and send the hunt to friends.

FAQ

  • Do players have to pay? No. Anyone who receives a hunt can play it to the end without buying anything.
  • The chest doesn’t appear (indoors). Point the camera slowly at the painting or poster used as the landmark, straight on and in good light. A printed marker also works.
  • I arrived outdoors but the chest won’t open. Locations are approximate. Wait a moment where you can see the sky, or ask the creator to make the arrival radius larger.
  • How do I send a hunt? Tap “Share” on the hunt to send the hunt file (.arhunt) by AirDrop, messaging apps, or email.
  • I want my purchases on another device. Tap “Restore purchases” in the Shop or Settings (use the same store account).
  • How do I cancel HUNTMIO Pass? On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Play safely

  • Don’t look at your screen while walking.
  • Stay out of roads and restricted areas.
  • Kids should play with a grown-up.
