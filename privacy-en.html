Privacy Policy

Operator: KEISUKE TOMONAGA

Contact: HUNTMIO-support@tetra2585.com

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. Our approach

HUNTMIO (Japanese name: AR宝探しクリエイター; “the app”) has no server of its own, no sign-in, no analytics tools, and no ads. The hunts you make and your play progress are stored only on your device.

2. Information used only on your device

  • Camera: The camera feed is processed on your device to show and place treasure chests in AR. The feed is not saved or sent. However, if a creator takes a photo of something in the room (such as a painting or poster) to use as a landmark, that photo is saved as part of the hunt (see section 3).
  • Room scan (when “No landmark” is chosen on iPhone): To show chests in the same spots, the app saves feature points describing the shape of the room on your device. This is not included in hunt files and is not sent anywhere.
  • Location: Used only while the app is in use, in outdoor mode, to show the distance to each spot and check when you arrive. It is never collected in the background, and it is not saved or sent (except spots a creator chooses to register in a hunt).
  • Photo library: Used only when a creator chooses a photo to put inside a chest. Only the chosen photo is resized and saved in the hunt; no other photos are read.
  • Microphone: Used only when a creator records a voice message for a chest (up to 30 seconds). The recording is saved only inside the hunt and is not sent anywhere.
  • Opening videos (iPhone): Only when a player turns on “Record video”, the app records its own screen and app sound and makes a short video of the moment a chest opens. The microphone is not used. The video is made on your device and leaves it only if you choose to share or save it.
  • Hunts, progress, and settings: Stored in a database and files on your device.

3. Information included in hunt files

When a creator exports a hunt file (.arhunt), it contains the hunt’s title and description, riddle answers and codes, messages, attached photos and voice messages, landmark photos for indoor hunts, and the latitude/longitude and descriptions of outdoor spots. Hunt files are sent only when you share them yourself (for example, with the share sheet), and the people who receive them, or anyone they forward them to, may be able to read their contents. Please don’t include information that identifies you or the location of your home, and make sure landmark photos don’t show faces, addresses, or documents.

4. External services

To confirm and restore in-app purchases, the app uses the App Store / Google Play and the purchase management service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created automatically for each device and purchase information (the products purchased, purchase dates, store receipts, and similar). Your name, email address, and similar details are not sent. This information is used to confirm and restore purchases and to let the operator see overall sales and purchase counts (individuals are not identified). RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

The app does not track you, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not sell any information to third parties.

5. Children

Children playing outdoors should be accompanied by a parent or guardian. The app does not collect personal information from children.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete hunts from your hunt list. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Purchase information can be deleted by following the procedures of each store and RevenueCat.

7. Your rights

The operator of the app does not hold personal information that identifies you. If you wish to make a request under the laws where you live (such as the EU GDPR or the California CCPA), including requests for access, correction, or deletion, please use the contact above. You can also contact RevenueCat and the stores about purchase information.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page. If the Japanese and English versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.
